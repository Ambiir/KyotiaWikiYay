Lekti

Entropic Spirits

The Lekti are energy-consuming spirits that manifest physical bodies from surrounding materials. As spirits, they burn more energy than they consume passively from stars and planets, forcing them to create tangible forms to sustain their existence. Highly adaptable and self-serving, they can thrive in any environment—from barren wastelands to advanced civilizations where they integrate technology into their anatomy.

Habitat

expand_more

Lektis can live pretty much anywhere. They're spirits who feed on energy passively given off by stars, planets, and anything else. As spirits, they burn off more energy than they're consuming, so to maintain their life, they would find a planet they like and create a physical body from surrounding materials.

Knowing this, they can be found on any planet, even if it's incapable of sustaining life. They're very adaptable as their physical bodies grow in these extreme climates, as they're made from materials directly from their surroundings.

In active civilizations, their surrounding materials can also include technology, which can act as their heart and prolong their body's lifetime.

Appearance

expand_more

The appearance of a lekti's physical form generally resembles a reptile. Their proportions and properties may vary based on their environment.

They all have white eyes which lack pupils, four horns, and a sharp, exposed spinal skeleton. They can range from any colour in skin and any material, just as long as it's inorganic. Examples are: granite, iron, steel, plastic, gold, etc.

Most lektis are made just of crude material, although some in existing societies that possess a powered machine in their anatomy may also be able to emit light from their eyes.

Many known civilizations so far have humanoid dominant species, and lektis that grow in these environments will adapt to this appearance as well.

Spirit Form

Their spirit forms, however, aren't visible to the usual eye, and only beings that can sense spiritual energy can sense their presence. They often move close to the speed of light, and can be VERY hard to spot without a physical vessel.

anatomy

Anatomy

expand_more

Lektis don't possess the organs of a usual organism. Usually, they just have a central stomach and second cavity for the core of their spirit anywhere on the body, usually in the head, sometimes the chest.

They usually do not have a physical brain, as it is not needed for their survival. Due to the nature of their being, destruction of the physical body does not mean certain death. Though, the destruction of these bodies aren't all that preferred either.

Stomach Fluid

The dark fluid seen in their stomachs are not only to help process the material, but they can also be used as a defensive mechanism by spitting it out. This is the same fluid that flows around their body to repair the damaged parts of themselves, similar to blood in humans. It's pretty corrosive.

Lektis heal by consuming whatever food they're made of, and allowing their stomach fluid to filter out unwanted material. They can use multiple materials to heal different areas of their body, especially if the lekti has complex parts in their body.

They can also have technology in their bodies which can improve their thinking capacity, the lifespan of their body, and can even provide special abilities. However, they will have a few more vulnerabilities as a result.

Lektis can maintain a memory up to a certain length of time. Anywhere past approximately 1500 years will usually be forgotten, or mixed into newer memory. The process is a slow fading out, and older lektis are often used to it.

Due to how they generally have long/immortal lives as they are purely spirits, reproduction isn't common. But it is possible to ensure the species will last. When a lekti has absorbed plenty of energy in their spirit, they will use a significant fraction to produce an offspring spirit, usually into the land or material they find suitable for the offspring to start their first body cycle.

Creating offspring is seen as a power move among Lektis, as it shows that the spirit is confident enough in their energy reserves to create a life. This is usually done willingly, and if overdone, can result in the death of a lektis spirit.

If a lekti spirit does die, it simply fizzes out, and there is no "afterlife" that remains. This is extremely rare though, and only occurs if an external force is involved.

Behaviour

expand_more

Lektis aren't naturally social creatures, being a long-life, asexual, energy being. If a lekti is raised correctly in a society, it'll also adapt to the mannerisms that is desired by society. A young lekti raised by an older person will often imitate their actions, regardless of the harm it may cause.

Lektis often have a desire for power and equal exchange, and this is brought out when they begin to thrive. They have a strong sense of personal justice too, and will go out of their way to return what's given to them, good or bad.

They need to be taught language to properly communicate, as self-taught lektis will misinterpret some words they use, and often sound like the stereotypical caveman. However, expect the lektis to lie, especially if they've been lied to before. Lektis don't think for anyone else but themselves.

⚠️ Note on Morality

Lektis tend to struggle understanding human morals and the concept of rules. They aren't social creatures, and so they don't have the concept of working with others immediately hammered into their minds. They will likely ask "Why? What's stopping me? There is no consequence. Why do you say I can't do this?"

Bonds & Affection

expand_more

They don't carry the same emotive instincts as humans, and it's impossible for a truly romantic relationship to take place. Platonic relationships, however, are possible. But that often only show affection based on learned behaviour of the other party, as they naturally do not carry any of their own.

However, the case of two lektis forming a positive relationship isn't impossible. Though, they don't truly show affection to each other. It's usually a mutually beneficial relationship where they both take on different survival tasks that they're skilled in, and occasionally provide each other with some entertainment if they begin to thrive.

In some cases, often seen more in societies, a lekti may risk their life or sacrifice something (big or small) for someone who they've formed a bond with. A lekti who willingly does this means that they highly value the person they're doing it for. Though, they will expect something in return.

Culture

expand_more

Lekti's lack a specific culture, as they are not social creatures. However, integrated into society, they will adapt to that of the society's cultures. From there, they'll often choose what they want.

Most lektis will adapt to the appearance and culture of the power-dominant type, as they find the strength and authority to be appealing, though some also choose the lesser kinds, as some cultures may depict them to be leaders, or simply found the aesthetic of their choice to be appealing.

As for religion, the lektis will have a hard time believing in gods, no matter how they're raised.

public

Their Place in Lexina

expand_more

Upon the first few lektis coming across this planet and staying within it, they were quite welcoming of the new guests. Lexina already has a bit of a range for intelligent species, so they integrated quite well. The presence of technology makes it a great home for lektis to stay and thrive in, as they can maintain longer physical lives and absorb more energy in their spirit, as well as communicate effectively with the other species of this planet.

Their resilience in harsh situations and adaptability is a use the Lexians often take advantage of by raising them in more extreme environments, so they could assist in carrying out tasks otherwise impossible for people.

⚠️ Exploitation

Unfortunately, in this world, lektis are also known to have their body material be an easy source of already purified materials, and are often victim to violence as people attempt to harvest their tail and limbs to sell on an illegal market.
