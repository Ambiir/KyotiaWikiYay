Multiverse Havoc

Where reality tears at the seams.

An alternate universe similar to Kyotia, but plagued by a universal phenomenon called "Anomalies"—portals, rifts, and non-euclidean paths that spawn and disappear at random, all manifested through the chaotic magic of Arzira.

blur_on The Anomalies

These mysterious openings appear without warning and vanish just as unpredictably. They can manifest anywhere—in the middle of a crowded city, deep within ancient forests, or floating in the void of space. The magic of Arzira, the Embodiment of Chaos, is responsible for their existence, though whether they are intentional creations or chaotic byproducts remains unknown.

Anomaly Characteristics

all_inclusive Portals

Direct doorways to other locations, sometimes stable enough to pass through safely.

broken_image Rifts

Tears in reality that leak energy and matter between dimensions unpredictably.

route Non-Euclidean Paths

Spatial distortions where distance and direction no longer follow normal rules.

help Unknown Types

Other anomaly variations exist but remain poorly documented due to their danger.

public Destinations Unknown

These openings often take people to other planets, other realms, or even other galaxies entirely. The destination is never predictable—one moment you could step through and find yourself on a lush alien world, the next you might emerge in the cold vacuum of deep space or a realm of pure magic.

warning

⚠️ Extreme Danger Warning

Whether travelers return or not is indefinite. People are often warned against entering anomalies, as stepping through one is treated almost like a suicide gamble. Many who enter are never seen again. Those who do return often speak of years passing in what felt like moments—or moments stretching into lifetimes.

auto_awesome Arzira's Influence

The anomalies are a direct manifestation of Arzira's chaotic magic permeating through this universe. As the Embodiment of Chaos, Arzira's power creates these rifts in reality—whether as intentional acts, unconscious overflow of power, or simply the natural consequence of chaos existing in a structured universe. Some scholars believe the anomalies serve a purpose known only to Arzira. Others think they are simply the universe struggling to contain infinite chaos.
