Lexina

A world under Zyvire's influence, where mortal gods were born from the people themselves.

World Overview

Lexina is a world in the Allior System, orbiting the star Allion alongside its sister planets: Sacrin, Kesilic, Nexilon, the Crystal Ring, Zyxol, and Ocsila. It is primarily influenced by Zyvire, though the world maintains its own separate divisions — countries that help ensure the population is kept track of.

What makes Lexina unique is its gods: they were all once ordinary Lexian people who were forced into godhood. This has shaped the culture and spirituality of the world in profound ways, creating a society where divinity is not distant but deeply personal and often tragic.

Exalvus Innovative Corp.

expand_more

A research corporation powering the technological advances of all of Lexina. They work with biological, electrical, magical, and cosmological subjects — with subjects "let out" being the leading cause of "Population control."

Facility Types

Commercial

Stores and display buildings showing off products and selling newest inventions before signing builds off to other companies.

Office

Four types: Biological, Electrical, Medical, Magical, and Cosmos buildings. Handle customer service, directing shipments, accounting, documentation, and basic testing.

Research

Where most staff members reside. Typical (mostly) ethical laboratories with advanced equipment and customized rooms for various projects.

Containment

Obscure facilities containing unethical and incredibly dangerous experiments. Only heavily armored security and experienced staff can access. Serves as an alternative fate for death row prisoners to become guinea pigs.

Headquarters

Located in Nesilivia. An airborne building where most important decisions are made, secretive meetings are held, and where you're most likely to find Zytex. Contains a main meeting room, sleeping quarters, food storage for a decade, and a global control room.

The Mortal Gods

expand_more

Unlike other worlds where gods are ancient cosmic entities, Lexina's gods were all once ordinary Lexian people — mortals who were forced into godhood against their will. This unique origin has shaped the relationship between the divine and the mortal in profound ways.

These mortal-turned-gods carry the weight of their humanity with them, creating a pantheon that is deeply personal, often tragic, and intimately connected to the struggles of everyday Lexians.

"I didn't want this… but the universe brought me this path. I'll have to take it either way, and change how this treacherous place works."

— The Pupil
