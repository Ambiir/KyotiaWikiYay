Biography

Amber is a robot with the brain of a human, created by Arzira through extensive trial and error. She is one of several creations made by the god of chaos across multiple worlds and alternate universes.

Awakening

Amber woke up within a house in Protostar under her name, as well as an assigned occupation in society as a Civilian Patroller. She awoke with absolutely no knowledge of herself or the world around her. Through experience and local help, she managed to figure out most of her essentials — but that's just the basics. Her true worth and the full extent of her abilities remain to be discovered.

The Awakening of All Creations

Amber is not alone in her mysterious origins. Across Arzira's worlds, other creations have awakened — each in an early stage of their respective worlds. They all go on to learn about their worlds, their purpose, and conform to society.

At least, until they each began to notice one thing: they never really physically aged. They were all at least pseudo-immortal. As they figured out who they were and started to ponder their odd differences from the rest of society, the god takes notice of this growing awareness and takes control of them.

⚠️ Arzira's Marionettes

Why is it unfair? They're her creations, after all. Even with her being a god, even she doesn't have free will. The creations are tied up in Arzira's marionette strings. There's nothing they can do about it. The god was their creator, and they were to accept that and suffer.

…Well, if it's inevitable, might as well make some fun out of it.

Skyforce

After what could have been days, months, years, or even decades... a blue gemstone came to Amber one day. It urged her to enter an unknown universe, to an unknown dimension. She created the portal, but unlike the usual black, it appeared pink and purple.

She jumped through and saw many people having fun, led on an incredible adventure by one of them. But the gem told her to keep going. She jumped through a portal of red, arriving at an abandoned academy. Then through a portal of blue, landing at an assortment of floating islands.

Before her stood a human in green and a star-like being entering through a warp. They each had similar gems — a green one for the human, and a yellow one for the star being. She thought to herself that her own gem probably should've been black or white instead... but that would've looked pretty ugly in comparison to the others. It would've been lifeless and out of place.
