Biography

Ari is a 9' tall Priskan-Human with light green skin and lighter green stripes down her face. She has a burn scar under her hair from childhood. Ari has long green hair tied up in a ponytail with a yellow star hair clip. She wears a white bandana and a light and dark grey hoodie with dark green pants and shoes.

Early Life

Ari and her brother Ace were discarded lab subjects of an experiment from late 1995 to early 1998. They were abandoned on the streets and picked up by a couple who brought them to a cheap orphanage with horrible care. As toddlers, they were exposed to violence and neglect. She got a nasty injury from being thrown into a wall, leaving her with a scar she hides under her hair. After that, Ace protected her fiercely, though it meant she often had to watch violent fights. During this time, she met her friend Neitia, who was 2 years younger. But Neitia eventually left, and Ari never knew when they would meet again.

School Years

When she started school, she met another friend named Parker and tried to introduce him to Ace. Although separated from everyone at times, the time away made her too nervous to talk. In middle school, she often skipped presentations out of pure anxiety, leading to detention. During one detention, she met Leslie (Neitia's non-blood sister), who introduced her to Gavin and Bailey. They quickly became close friends. She was afraid to introduce them to her brother due to his nature, but when an incident happened and she had to visit Ace in the hospital, they insisted on going with her.

Adulthood

Ari now has a stable life as both a freelance accountant and working multirole in a small bakery. At 22, she found two lektis on the street and named them Sydney and Frostbite. She was okay as a parent, but perhaps too soft on them — though they seemed well mannered enough, or just really asocial.

Ari tends to be nervous and introverted, but she doesn't like having enemies. Even though she tries to be as kind and generous as possible, she doesn't like talking or being open to anyone who aren't her friends — though she hates being alone as well. Sometimes, her generosity can be a bit of a pushover and be self-destructive.
