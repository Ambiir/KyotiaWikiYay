Yinavi

The Realm of Imagination — a dimension shaped by dreams and thoughts, serving as the meeting point for the Kyotian Council of Gods. Guardian: Avris.

info Overview

Yinavi is one of the outer dimensional layers of the Kyotia universe. Think of the universe like an onion — layers that border the edge of existence. Yinavi was originally a blank white plain, but through the imagination and will of its guardian spirit Avris, it has been shaped into a realm of floating islands, ferris wheels, and countless homes built for dreamers who may never arrive.

Similar to Crysilact, Yinavi can sustain some life as well as energy beings, providing a stable settlement for spirits before they fade or burn. After the other gods discovered the outer realms, they designated Yinavi as the official meeting area for the Kyotian Council of Gods.

person Guardian Spirit: Avris

psychology

Origin of Avris

A spirit conjured from the residue energy that came with Arzira's creation of a new realm. Avris's consciousness became one with Yinavi, shaping the blank white void into a realm of imagination.

hearing

The Listener

Avris can hear the dreams, thoughts, and imaginations of all living beings across the universe. These whispers guide the design of Yinavi's landscapes, textures, and structures.

warning Conflict with Orion

When Avris finally made contact with the other gods after eons of solitude, another conflict stirred — this time between Avris and Orion. The nature of this disagreement remains a closely guarded secret among the Kyotian Council.
