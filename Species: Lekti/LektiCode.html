The Astrals

Guardians of the Starlight Realm

The Astrals are ancient, celestial humanoids who serve as the Guardians of the Starlight Realm. Born from nebulae and sustained by pure solar radiation, they act as mediators within the Kyotia alliance, bridging the ontological gap between the material plane and the ethereal currents of the cosmos. Their existence is a testament to the universe's ability to manifest consciousness from light itself.

Habitat

expand_more

The Astrals reside in floating crystalline cities, known as "Spires of Dawn," located within the upper stratosphere of Kyotia Prime. These massive, translucent structures are tethered to the planet's magnetic field via gravity-anchors and drift in synchronization with the polar auroras.

Appearance & Anatomy

expand_more

Astrals are characterized by their tall, slender frames and semi-translucent skin that shifts in hue depending on their emotional state and local star proximity. Instead of blood, a luminous fluid resembling liquid starlight circulates through their visible nervous system. They lack traditional facial features like mouths or noses, communicating entirely through telepathy, though they possess large, opalescent eyes.

Life Cycle

expand_more

Astrals do not reproduce biologically in the traditional sense. A new Astral is "woven" from a convergence of high-energy cosmic rays in the Stellar Nursery. They emerge fully formed but with a blank consciousness, spending their first 500 cycles (The Dim Era) absorbing knowledge from the collective. Upon death, their physical form dissipates into photons, returning their energy to the nearest star.

Culture & Society

expand_more

Astral society is a meritocratic hierarchy based on "Luminosity"—a measure of one's wisdom and contribution to the preservation of cosmic balance. They are pacifists by nature but wield devastating light-based magic in defense of the realm. Art is created through light-weaving, sculpting hard-light holograms that can last for millennia.
