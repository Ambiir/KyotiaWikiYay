Biography

Across Lexina, 12 children spawned from fragments that might've come from the crystals hidden deep in the moons — or perhaps scattered crystals planted by gods. Whatever it may be, it created the 12 demigods, each with a gift, and immortality.

The Gift Awakens

Oculi's gift is the ability to communicate with the spiritual realm — an additional sense that allows her to see magic. It was faint whispers and voices at first, but began to set in more as she grew. It fully came to be when she was about 25, where her body also stopped aging.

Hacia

Oculi had a wonderful wife at the time — Hacia. It was the only person she cared about. The only person that loved Oculi back so dearly, despite those dark eyes like the void. But that was short-lived, for a mortal only lives for a small fraction of an immortal's life.

Same-sex marriage back then was a weird topic, and it didn't help that Oculi's dark eyes were seen as a curse. Not many people knew about their relationship.

Descent into Madness

After Hacia's passing from old age, Oculi started trying to talk to her spirit, but Hacia wanted for Oculi to move on. Even spirits aren't forever, after all — and attempting to make these weaker spirits last only causes them to burn and suffer.

Oculi still tried to seek out Hacia, going as far as to ask other spirits for her whereabouts. This is when some ill-intentioned people started feeding her lies and making her do crimes as favours, hence her eventual decline of sanity.

⚠️ Warning: Dark Content

Around the 1690s, when each of the lunar demigods started meeting each other (as all the friends and family they've known in their early life were dead), Nova was the one to notice Oculi first — while Oculi was in the middle of murdering someone for a vengeful spirit.

Cat and Mouse

Nova tried to catch her, as the just person they see themselves to be, but Oculi had fled the scene. After that came a series of events where they were basically in a cat-and-mouse chase, with Oculi performing increasingly more violent acts each time — going as far as to set a federal building on fire.

When Oculi was finally caught, despite all her atrocities, she pleaded insanity. It wasn't that hard for her to plead insanity, given that she already whispers, mutters, and talks to ghosts. And so she ended up in the asylum for the next couple hundred years.

Forgotten & Found

She was basically forgotten there after a while, but Nova noticed she was still alive and... well, hadn't aged. Nova hadn't been aging either, as another one of the lunar demigods.

So Nova decided to break Oculi out and bring her to the forests as a safe area. Oculi can barely speak at all at this point because she's been isolated for so long.
