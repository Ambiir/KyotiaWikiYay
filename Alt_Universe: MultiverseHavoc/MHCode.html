The Shattered Rift

Where the laws of physics are mere suggestions.

The Shattered Rift represents a divergence point in the timeline roughly 3,000 cycles prior to the Unification Era. In this reality, the containment field surrounding the Primordial Singularity collapsed, fragmenting the material plane into millions of floating islands suspended in a void of raw arcane energy.

history_edu Historical Divergence

Unlike Prime Kyotia, where the Astrals stabilized the planet's core, the Rift's version of the Astral Council attempted to harness the singularity's power directly. The resulting feedback loop—known as The Great Splintering—shattered the planet's crust. Gravity is no longer a constant force but varies depending on proximity to the jagged "Ley-Shards" that drift through the atmosphere.

cyclone The Void-Sea

Between the floating remnants of civilization lies the Void-Sea, a nebular ocean of purple and midnight blue gas. It is home to mutated fauna that have adapted to vacuum exposure. The sentient species of this world travel between islands on "Skimmers"—ships rigged with solar sails that catch the currents of starlight. Survival here demands constant movement, as staying in one location too long risks being caught in a reality tremor.
