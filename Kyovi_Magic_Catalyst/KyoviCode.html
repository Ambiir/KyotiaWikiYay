Origins of the Kyovi

The Kyovi magic system predates the Great Shattering by three millennia. Originally discovered by the Ancient Seers of the Sapphire Void, it relies not on the physical laws of the universe, but on the resonant frequencies of the soul.

While physical energy is finite and bound by entropy, Kyovi energy is drawn from the infinite wellspring of the Astral Plane. However, the conduit—the mortal mind—is fragile. The first practitioners learned quickly that drawing too deeply from the wellspring resulted not in power, but in Crystalline Madness, a state where the body turns to unmelting sapphire while the mind remains eternally conscious.
