Biography

Ace is a 9'3" tall Priskan with short, black hair and grey skin, featuring two notable white stripes going down his face. His eyes are white, and he has a stitched wound over his fake left eye. Numerous scars cover his body. He's known for wearing his purple headband with a grey crescent moon hair clip and his grey hoodie, usually paired with purple pants and shoes.

Early Life

Ace and his sister, Ari, were discarded lab subjects of an experiment in late 1995 to early 1998. They were abandoned on the streets in the division of Tseki and picked up by a couple who brought them to the only orphanage available — a particularly cheap one with harsh conditions. When he saw how badly his younger sister got hurt, he swore to protect her under any circumstances, often fighting back with whatever gnarly tactic he could think of. As a result, Ace grew up with violent tendencies, exposed to violence and neglect. He was feared and often alone, though the loneliness never bothered him.

School Years

In school, he wouldn't talk to anyone and hated separating from Ari. He met his first friend, Parker, here — someone who put up with his violent sociopathic tendencies and always spoke his mind. During middle school, he became the talk of gossip as the "quiet kid." A hallway fight ended with him killing a classmate, leaving him with a missing eye and stitches in his head. At 15, he met Leslie, Gavin, Bailey, and Neitia (Ari's friend from the orphanage) when Ari visited him in the hospital.

Adulthood

Ace moved on to have a semi-stable life, working as a bodyguard. At 17, he took in a blue lekti named Xenoid, and later an orange stellrix already named Kashi. He also has a dark purple cat named Ceres. At 25, during a trip to the famous frozen Crystal Lake in southern Naneka, a massive riot occurred. They were caught in the chaos of fire and bullets — Leslie was shot and died.

Present Day

Now in his mid-30s, Ace has repaired friendships with Gavin and Parker, while Bailey "tolerates" him. Around 37, he lost his right arm on the job when a gang attacked — a needle or dart caused his arm to rot, requiring amputation. He replaced it with a long blade for work. Things are finally looking bright and hopeful.

Ace is usually reserved and introverted, but that doesn't stop him from showing his thoughts through his expressions. With people he's forced to work with, he's rude, insensitive, and pokes mean fun. Only with those incredibly close to him does he show more sympathy and openness.
