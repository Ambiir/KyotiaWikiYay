Divine History

At the dawn of the universe, two spirits came to be — Chaos and Order. The Order immediately began observing the universe and its laws. The explosions, the heat, and the chaos of the beginning overwhelmed him. He hated where he found himself to be. He wanted a way to stop.

Birth & The Banishment

That's when he looked to Arzira. The Chaos. He saw her existence as a plague — the cause of all this unbearable noise. He wanted her gone. So he began to experiment with time and space itself, despite the headaches it caused him. He found what came naturally, and what didn't. He traveled out to the distant realms, uncovering all that existed at the time: the Static Plains, the Kyovi, the Isles, and Reality's Collapse. A chaotic place for a chaotic beast. With this in mind, he returned and approached Arzira, to rid of the chaotic pest and banish her.

The Shattering

After Arzira's unconsensual departure, there was nothing in the universe but the ever-changing cycle of stars and himself. He was responsible for keeping order in this universe, and that weighed on him. If he were to run this, perhaps it would be easier to be more than one being. So he split himself in three, all to different parts of the universe. It felt better. It was better, and he hoped for it to stay this way. Unfortunately, things changed. The minds of his split forms did not follow him, and their views began to drift. The Order's Fragments of Space, Energy, and Time were made. The Fragment of Space renamed themselves to Kyomu, and the Fragment of Energy renamed himself to Kyovoi.

Present Day

Amano didn't know what to do, so he froze himself in time, to avoid all of this. He shut himself in the Static Plains, where he can't reach Chaos, and Chaos can't reach him. That is until Arzira found him, and ripped him out of stasis. A mere fragment of the Order is no longer a threat to the Chaos, and Arzira was ecstatic to see the vermin she despised fall into such disrepair. So she spun her blue strings, and chained the Order down to her wants and will, and he can't do anything about it.

Amano is a being who preaches of truth and order. He prefers to sit in silence, and often gets overwhelmed or irritated by noise and action. He's extremely blunt and doesn't act on any extra emotions, if at all. Amano tends to use the most basic or accurate terminology possible to explain anything.

— Nature of Order
