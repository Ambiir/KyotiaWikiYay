Divine History

At the dawn of the universe, two spirits came to be — Chaos and Order. Chaos was a curious little thing, always wandering across the stars, tinkering with galaxies. All this novelty was heaven for her! She shared all that she saw with the Order, and frequently flew off far away to try pushing the limits of the universe.

Birth & Banishment

Many eons passed, and the universe remained in a chaotic state of stars constantly collapsing and conceiving. Arzira was watching the stars come and go, when Amano, the Order, approached her and claimed to banish her. Arzira still believed they were equals, thinking the Order was merely a bore. But Amano had already struck, permanently corrupting a part of her soul into a constant state of fluctuations and banishing her to the edge of the universe: Reality's Collapse.

Exile in Reality's Collapse

Arzira was banished to the edge of the universe, which took form of a labyrinth of countless passages that overlap and cross in ways which can twist one's mind into insanity. Reality's Collapse was its name. Each passage has many holes, doors, and traps that lead to other spaces and states of existence — whether within the universe or outside of it. Arzira searched and explored, seeking a way back, but couldn't find one. So she embraced the outer realm as her own and began to study her own spirit like Amano did, for yet another eternity. Eventually, she lost her need to return to the core of Kyotia. She created countless alternate universes, outside of Amano's knowledge, where she's free.

Present Day

When she finally decided to return to the original universe's center, she found Amano to be in fragments of what he once was. It was satisfying. So satisfying. She could puppet and dance with the very thing that sent her away. Her revenge? Taking Amano away from his other two fragments, so they could develop minds of their own. She took him to the alternate universes she made — her stages, her plays — all with mortals that have slightly different storylines. She even took him beyond Kyotia, where their powers start to weaken as they stray far from the Kyovi's source.

Arzira relishes in chaos and lies, often appearing on a whim with a cheerful air. She often takes on an actress's persona, and imitates the appearance of common folk to drive along the plot of someone's life. She's easily bored by stale moments and uneventful times, and will always seek out a good story or an era of chaos.

— Nature of Chaos
